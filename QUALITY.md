# Quality and registry

Official: project-owned/commissioned content editorially approved. Verified: provenance and factual review completed by maintainers at exact pinned bytes. Community: structurally valid and rights attested; editorial review pending. These are not university endorsements, grade or mastery claims. Only maintainers grant labels; each update needs a new receipt.

Creators may host their own repositories. Propose course ID, HTTPS URL, immutable full commit, version, bundle path and SHA-256, compatibility, author/license and per-asset provenance. Tags alone are insufficient. Registry review pins bytes and records reviewer/date/quality; updates use PRs. Fail closed on digest mismatch, unsafe archives or unsupported format. Never forward app credentials. Preserve earlier pins for rollback; revoke unsafe catalog entries with notice. The registry contains only the synthetic draft demo; ingestion remains pending. See [FORMAT.md](docs/FORMAT.md) and [REVIEW.md](docs/REVIEW.md). The v1 statuses draft/in-review/reviewed track review progress; they do not award the editorial Official/Verified/Community labels above. No quality label or real review is claimed for the demo. Remote entries remain draft proposals until locally vendored and reviewed.
