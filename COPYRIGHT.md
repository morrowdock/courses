# Copyright and takedown

Submit only content you may redistribute under CC BY-SA 4.0. Provide author, sources, per-asset hash/rights holder/license, permission evidence and change attribution. No instructor sheets, textbook problems, Canvas exports or learner uploads. Attribution alone is insufficient.

Private staging reports go to the project owner via the existing private channel. Before release, designate a confidential copyright contact and enforcement owner. Reports identify version/URL, claimed work, rights basis and reply route. Maintainers may delist pending review, privately notify the creator, preserve private evidence, correct/remove affected pins and permit appeal to another maintainer. Do not publish personal details. Valid CC grants cannot be retroactively revoked by delisting. Statutory safe-harbor status is not asserted.
